1. System Overview

1.1 What is the Template Masterfile?

The Template Masterfile is a tool built into Google Sheets that helps you organize and create grade sheets for your school. Think of it as a central hub where you can:

  • Assign which teachers teach which subjects to which classes
  • Assign which teachers are advisers for which classes
  • Create official grade sheet templates automatically

The system does all the heavy work for you - you just need to fill in the information through simple forms.

1.2 Quick Start Guide

To assign subjects to teachers:

  1. Open your spreadsheet → Click Manage at the top → Choose Manage Subjects
  2. Pick the grade level, section, and teacher's name
  3. Check the boxes next to the subjects and click Add Subject

To assign advisory classes:

  1. Open your spreadsheet → Click Manage at the top → Choose Manage Advisory Classes
  2. Pick the teacher, grade level, and section
  3. Click Add Advisory

To create a grade sheet template:

  1. Open your spreadsheet → Click Export OGS at the top → Choose Export OGS
  2. Type in the school year, then pick the grade level, section, and teacher
  3. Check that the subjects are correct and click Generate Template

2. Manage Subjects Instructions

2.1 Opening the Manage Subjects Form

  1. Open the Google Sheets file for your school's Template Masterfile
  2. Look at the top of the screen and click on Manage
  3. Click Manage Subjects from the list that appears
  4. A pop-up window will appear with the form

2.2 Assigning Subjects to Teachers

Step 1: Choose the Grade Level

  1. Click on the box labeled Grade Level
  2. A list will drop down - click on the grade level you want (like Grade 7, etc.)

Step 2: Choose the Section

  1. After you pick a grade level, the Section box will now show options
  2. Click on it and choose the section (like Section A, Section B, etc.)

Step 3: Choose the Teacher

  1. Click in the box labeled Teacher
  2. Start typing the teacher's name
  3. As you type, matching names will appear below - click on the correct name
  4. You can also use the up and down arrow keys on your keyboard to scroll through the names, then press Enter to select

Note: You'll only see teachers who are marked as "active" in your teacher list.

Step 4: Choose the Subjects

  1. You'll see a list of subjects with empty boxes next to them
  2. Click the boxes next to each subject you want to assign to this teacher
  3. You can select as many subjects as you need

Remember: All the subjects in this list are the ones marked as "active" in your SUBJECTS_REF sheets. The subjects you check will be assigned to the teacher for this specific grade level and section.

Step 5: Save the Assignment

  1. When you're ready, click the blue Add Subject button at the bottom
  2. The button will change to say Adding... while the system works
  3. If you selected multiple subjects, it will say Adding X assignment(s)... (where X is the number of subjects)
  4. Wait for a message that says "Success!" or confirms the assignment was added

Step 6: What Happens Next

After the assignment is saved:

  1. The subjects you selected are now assigned to that teacher for that grade level and section
  2. The list of subjects will refresh to show the new assignments
  3. The form will clear out so you can add more assignments if needed

2.3 Viewing Subject Assignments

How to See Existing Assignments

  1. Click on the Grade Level box and choose a grade level
  2. Click on the Section box and choose a section
  3. The list below will automatically show all the subjects that are currently assigned for that grade level and section
  4. Each line shows: The teacher's name and the subject they teach

Understanding What You See

  • You'll only see assignments that are currently active (not archived/deleted ones)
  • Each line = one teacher teaching one subject
  • The list updates automatically every time you change the grade level or section

2.4 Removing Subject Assignments

Step 1: Choose What to Remove

  1. Look at the list of subject assignments
  2. Click the box next to each assignment you want to remove
  3. You can select as many as you need
  4. There's a Select All option if you want to remove everything shown
  5. As you click boxes, the count at the top will update to show how many you've selected

Step 2: Delete the Assignments

  1. Click the red Delete Selected button
  2. A message will pop up asking "Are you sure?" - click to confirm
  3. The button will change to say Deleting X... (where X is how many you're deleting)
  4. Wait for a message confirming the deletion

Step 3: What Happens Next

After deletion:

  • The assignments you selected will be removed from view
  • The list will refresh to show what's left
  • Don't worry - the assignments aren't completely erased, they're just archived (hidden)

2.5 Error Messages and Solutions

Can't Save - Missing Information

What this means: You tried to click Add Subject but didn't fill in everything needed.

How to fix it:

  1. Make sure you've selected a grade level
  2. Make sure you've selected a section
  3. Make sure you've typed in a teacher's name
  4. Make sure you've checked at least one subject box
  5. Then try clicking Add Subject again
Subject Already Assigned to Someone Else

What this means: You're trying to assign a subject to a teacher, but that subject is already assigned to a different teacher for the same grade level and section.

Why this happens: The system doesn't allow two teachers to teach the same subject to the same class.

How to fix it:

  1. First, remove the existing assignment (the message will tell you which one)
  2. Then come back and add the new assignment
  3. Or, if you made a mistake, just assign a different subject instead
Forgot to Check Any Subjects

What this means: You clicked Add Subject but forgot to check any subject boxes.

How to fix it:

  1. Scroll through the subjects list
  2. Click the boxes next to the subjects you want to assign
  3. You must check at least one box
  4. Then click Add Subject again
Can't Delete Anything

What this means: The Delete Selected button is grayed out and you can't click it.

Why this happens: There are no assignments to delete for the grade level and section you're viewing.

How to fix it:

  1. Make sure you've selected the correct grade level and section
  2. Check if there are actually any assignments showing in the list
  3. Make sure you've checked at least one box next to an assignment before trying to delete

3. Manage Advisory Classes Instructions

3.1 Opening the Manage Advisory Classes Form

  1. Open the Google Sheets file for your Template Masterfile
  2. At the top of the screen, click on Manage
  3. Click Manage Advisory Classes from the list
  4. A pop-up window will appear with the form

3.2 Assigning Advisory Classes to Teachers

Step 1: Choose the Teacher

  1. Click in the Teacher box
  2. Start typing the teacher's name
  3. Matching names will appear below - click on the correct one
  4. Or use your arrow keys to scroll through the names and press Enter

Note: Only active teachers will appear in this list.

Step 2: Choose the Grade Level

  1. Click on the Grade Level box
  2. A list will drop down - click on the grade level (like Grade 1, etc.)
  3. After you select this, the Section box will become available

Step 3: Choose the Section

  1. Click on the Section box (it should now be clickable)
  2. Choose the section (like Section A, Section B, etc.)
  3. After you select this, the list below will show if this teacher already has any advisory classes

Step 4: Save the Advisory Assignment

  1. Click the blue Add Advisory button
  2. The button will change to say Adding... while the system works
  3. Wait for a confirmation message

Step 5: What Happens Next

After saving:

  • The teacher is now assigned as the adviser for that grade level and section
  • The list of advisories will refresh to show the new assignment
  • The form will clear so you can add more if needed

3.3 Viewing Advisory Assignments

How to See What a Teacher Advises

  1. Click in the Teacher box and type or select a teacher's name
  2. The list below will automatically show all the advisory classes for that teacher
  3. Each line shows the grade level and section (like "Grade 1A" or "Grade 7B")

Understanding What You See

  • You'll only see advisory assignments that are currently active
  • Each line = one grade level and section that this teacher advises
  • The list updates automatically when you change the teacher

3.4 Removing Advisory Assignments

Step 1: Choose What to Remove

  1. First, select a teacher to see their advisory assignments
  2. Click the box next to each advisory you want to remove
  3. You can select multiple advisories
  4. Use Select All if you want to remove all of them
  5. The count will update to show how many you've selected

Step 2: Delete the Advisories

  1. Click the red Delete Selected button
  2. A confirmation message will appear - click to confirm
  3. The button will change to say Deleting... while it works
  4. Wait for a success message

Step 3: What Happens Next

After deletion:

  • The advisories you selected will be removed from view
  • The list will refresh automatically
  • The advisories are archived, not permanently deleted

3.5 Error Messages and Solutions

Can't Save - Missing Information

What this means: You clicked Add Advisory but didn't fill in all the required information.

How to fix it:

  1. Make sure you've selected a teacher
  2. Make sure you've selected a grade level
  3. Make sure you've selected a section
  4. All three must be filled in before you can save
This Section Already Has an Adviser

What this means: You're trying to assign a teacher as adviser, but that grade level and section already has a different teacher assigned as adviser.

Why this happens: Each class can only have one adviser.

How to fix it:

  1. First, remove the existing advisory assignment
  2. Then come back and add the new one
  3. Or check if you selected the wrong section by mistake
No Advisories to Remove

What this means: There are no advisory assignments to delete.

Why this happens: The teacher you selected doesn't have any active advisory assignments.

What to do:

  1. Check if you selected the correct teacher
  2. If this teacher shouldn't have any advisories, then everything is correct
  3. You won't be able to delete anything if there's nothing to delete

4. Generate OGS Template Instructions

4.1 Opening the Generate OGS Template Form

  1. Open the Google Sheets file for your Template Masterfile
  2. At the top of the screen, click on Export OGS
  3. Click Export OGS from the list
  4. A pop-up window will appear with the form

4.2 Creating an OGS Template

Step 1: Type the School Year

  1. Click in the School Year box
  2. Type the school year using this format: "YYYY-YYYY"
  3. For example, type "2024-2025" for school year 2024-2025
  4. Make sure to include the hyphen (-) between the years

Important: This field is required - you must fill it in.

Step 2: Choose the Grade Level

  1. Click on the Grade Level box
  2. Select the grade level (like Grade 1, Grade 7, Grade 11, etc.)
  3. After you select this, the Section box will become available
  4. The Teacher box will stay grayed out until you also select a section

Step 3: Choose the Section

  1. Click on the Section box (it should now be clickable)
  2. Select the section (like A, B, C, etc.)
  3. The list only shows sections that belong to your selected grade level
  4. After you select this, the Teacher box will become available

Step 4: Choose the Teacher

  1. Click in the Teacher box
  2. Start typing the teacher's name
  3. You'll see matching names appear - these are teachers who teach the grade level and section you selected
  4. Click on the correct name or use arrow keys and press Enter

Note: You'll only see teachers who have subjects assigned to them for this specific grade level and section.

Step 5: Review the Subjects

  1. After you select a teacher, a section called Assigned Subjects will appear
  2. This shows all the subjects that teacher teaches for this grade level and section
  3. Check this list carefully to make sure all the expected subjects are there
  4. If a subject is missing, you'll need to go back and assign it first using the Manage Subjects form

Step 6: Create the Template

  1. When everything looks correct, click the green Generate Template button
  2. The button will change to say Generating... while the system works
  3. This might take a few moments - please wait
  4. A success message will appear with a link to your new template
  5. The template file is automatically saved to your Google Drive

Step 7: What Happens Next

After the template is created:

  1. Your template is saved in Google Drive

  1. A record is added to a special sheet called MASTER_DATA sheet
  2. This record includes a link you can click to open the template anytime
  3. The template includes separate sheets for each subject the teacher teaches

4.3 Error Messages and Solutions

Can't Generate - Missing Information

What this means: You clicked Generate Template but didn't fill in all the required information.

How to fix it:

  1. Make sure you typed in the school year
  2. Make sure you selected a grade level
  3. Make sure you selected a section
  4. Make sure you selected a teacher
  5. All four must be filled in before you can generate a template
Template Already Exists

What this means: A template file has already been created with this exact same combination of school year, grade level, section, and teacher.

Why this matters: The system won't create duplicate templates to avoid confusion.

What to do:

  1. The error message will show you a link to the existing template - click it to open that template
  2. If you want to create a new one anyway, you'll need to delete the old template file first from Google Drive
  3. Or, you can just use and edit the existing template instead
No Teacher Available

What this means: There are no teachers assigned to teach any subjects for the grade level and section you selected.

Why this happens: The system can't create a template if there's no teacher assigned.

How to fix it:

  1. Go back to the Manage Subjects form first
  2. Assign at least one subject to a teacher for this grade level and section
  3. Then come back to this form and try again
  4. The Teacher box will remain grayed out until you make those assignments

5. Managing Your Data

5.1 Reference Shee

The system uses several sheets in your spreadsheet where you can add or update information directly. Think of these as your master lists.

SUBJECTS_REF Sheet

What it's for: This sheet contains all the subjects available in your school.

How to use it:

  1. To add a new subject, simply add a new row with the subject name
  2. In the "Active" column, put a checkmark (✓) to make the subject available in the forms
  3. Remove the checkmark to hide a subject (when you don't need it anymore but want to keep the record)
  4. Only subjects marked as active will show up when you're assigning teachers to subjects

TEACHERS_REF Sheet

What it's for: This sheet contains all the teachers in your school.

How to use it:

  1. To add a new teacher, add a new row with the teacher's name
  2. In the "Active" column, put a checkmark (✓) to make the teacher available in the forms
  3. Remove the checkmark for teachers who are no longer active (like those who left) but you want to keep their records
  4. Only active teachers will appear in the forms

SECTIONS_REF Sheet

What it's for: This sheet lists all grade levels and sections in your school.

How to use it:

  1. To add a new section, add a new row with the grade level and section name
  2. The "Level" column (Elementary/JHS/SHS) is filled in automatically based on the grade level
  3. When you're filling out forms, sections are filtered to match the grade level you select

GRADING_REF Sheet

What it's for: This sheet controls how grades are calculated for each subject.

How to use it:

  1. Each row sets the percentages for Written Work, Performance Task, and Assessment
  2. If a subject has its own row, those percentages will be used for that subject
  3. The "DEFAULT" row is used for subjects that don't have their own specific row
  4. Only rows marked as active are used
  5. Make sure the three percentages add up to 100%

Important: Don't delete the DEFAULT row - the system needs it as a backup.

ATTENDANCE_REF Sheet

What it's for: This sheet contains the standardized number of school days per month and per school year.

How to use it:

  • This sheet typically doesn't need to be edited frequently
  • Only update if your school calendar changes

CHARACTERS_REF Sheet

What it's for: This sheet lists character traits used for grading student character and behavior.

How to use it:

  1. Add character traits as needed
  2. Mark rows as active or inactive using the Active column
  3. Only active rows are used in the system

5.2 System Sheets (Do Not Edit These)

These sheets are managed automatically by the system. It's best not to edit them directly because the system uses them to keep track of everything.

MASTER_DATA Sheet

What it's for: This sheet keeps a record of every template file you've created.

What it contains:

  • School Year
  • Grade Level
  • Section
  • Teacher name
  • A link to the template file (click to open it)
  • When it was created and last modified
  • Who created it

How to use it: Just use the links to find and open your template files. Don't edit the data directly.

SUBJECTS Sheet

What it's for: This sheet stores all the subject assignments (which teacher teaches what to which class).

What it contains:

  • Every subject assignment you've made through the Manage Subjects form
  • Status showing if it's Active or Archived
  • Tracking information about when and who made changes

How to use it: Let the Manage Subjects form handle all updates. Don't edit this sheet directly.

ADVISORY Sheet

What it's for: This sheet stores all the advisory class assignments (which teacher advises which class).

What it contains:

  • Every advisory assignment you've made through the Manage Advisory Classes form
  • Status showing if it's Active or Archived
  • Tracking information about when and who made changes

How to use it: Let the Manage Advisory Classes form handle all updates. Don't edit this sheet directly.

5.3 Where Your Templates are Saved

File Organization

Where to find your templates:

  1. All templates are automatically saved to your Google Drive
  2. Each template file has a specific name that includes the school year, grade level, section, and teacher
  3. Each template file contains multiple sheets inside - one sheet for each subject

How to access templates:

  • Click the links in the MASTER_DATA sheet, or
  • Go to your Google Drive and search for the file name
  • You can also find the link in the success message when you first create the template

6. Troubleshooting Guide

6.1 Forms Won't Open

Problem: When you click on a menu item, the form doesn't appear or you see an error message.

Try these solutions:

  1. Check your internet: Make sure you're connected to the internet
  2. Refresh the page: Press F5 on your keyboard or click the refresh button in your browser
  3. Close and reopen: Close the form if it's partially open, then try opening it again from the menu
  4. Check permissions: Make sure you have permission to edit the spreadsheet (ask your administrator if you're not sure)
  5. Contact support: If none of these work, contact your systems developer

6.2 Template Won't Create

Problem: You clicked Generate Template but it didn't work or showed an error.

Try these solutions:

  1. Check subject assignments: Make sure the teacher has subjects assigned for the selected grade level and section (use Manage Subjects to check)
  2. Check teacher status: Go to the TEACHERS_REF sheet and make sure the teacher has a checkmark (✓) in the Active column
  3. Check grading weights: Go to the GRADING_REF sheet and make sure there's a DEFAULT row that's marked as active
  4. Check all fields: Make sure you filled in school year, grade level, section, and teacher
  5. Check Google Drive: Make sure you have permission to create files in Google Drive
  6. Contact support: If it still doesn't work, contact your systems developer

6.3 Can't Assign Subjects

Problem: You're trying to assign a subject but it won't let you, or you see an error message.

Try these solutions:

  1. Check if subject is active: Go to the SUBJECTS_REF sheet and make sure the subject has a checkmark (✓) in the Active column
  2. Check for conflicts: The error message will tell you if the subject is already assigned to a different teacher for the same grade level and section - you need to remove that assignment first
  3. Check teacher status: Go to the TEACHERS_REF sheet and make sure the teacher has a checkmark (✓) in the Active column
  4. Check selections: Make sure you've selected a grade level, section, teacher, and at least one subject

6.4 Can't Assign Advisory Classes

Problem: You're trying to assign an advisory but it won't let you, or you see an error message.

Try these solutions:

  1. Check for existing adviser: The error will tell you if that grade level and section already has a different teacher as adviser - you need to remove that assignment first
  2. Check teacher status: Go to the TEACHERS_REF sheet and make sure the teacher has a checkmark (✓) in the Active column
  3. Check selections: Make sure you've selected a teacher, grade level, and section

6.5 Dropdown Lists are Empty

Problem: When you click on a dropdown (like Grade Level or Section), nothing appears, or the list is empty.

Try these solutions:

  1. Check reference sheets: Make sure the SUBJECTS_REF, TEACHERS_REF, and SECTIONS_REF sheets have data in them
  2. Check active status: Make sure the items in those sheets have checkmarks (✓) in their Active column
  3. Check sheet structure: Make sure these sheets have 2 header rows at the top (the system expects this)
  4. Refresh the form: Close the form and open it again
  5. Check spelling: Sheet names must be spelled exactly right (including capital letters)

6.6 Teacher Names Don't Appear

Problem: When you type in the Teacher box, no suggestions appear, or the wrong teachers appear.

Try these solutions:

  1. Check TEACHERS_REF: Go to the TEACHERS_REF sheet and make sure the teacher exists and has a checkmark (✓) in the Active column
  2. Select grade/section first: In the Generate OGS form, you must select a grade level and section before teacher names will appear
  3. Check spelling: Make sure you're typing the name correctly
  4. Refresh the form: Close and reopen the form
  5. Check assignments: In the Generate OGS form, only teachers who have subjects assigned for that specific grade level and section will appear

6.7 Can't Find My Template File

Problem: You created a template but can't find it now.

Try these solutions:

  1. Check MASTER_DATA sheet: Open the MASTER_DATA sheet in your spreadsheet and look for the template - there's a link you can click
  2. Click the link: In the Template Link column, click on "Open Template" to go directly to the file
  3. Check Google Drive access: Make sure you have access to the Google Drive folder where templates are saved
  4. Search in Drive: Go to Google Drive and search for part of the file name (like the teacher's name or grade level)
  5. Check success message: When you first created the template, the success message included a link - check if you still have that message open

6.8 Wrong Grading Weights

Problem: The template is using incorrect percentages for Written Work, Performance Task, and Assessment.

Try these solutions:

  1. Check GRADING_REF sheets: Go to the GRADING_REF sheet and look for the subject
  2. Check spelling: Make sure the subject name is spelled exactly the same way (including capital letters and spaces)
  3. Check active status: Make sure the row has a checkmark (✓) in the Active column
  4. Check DEFAULT row: Make sure there's a DEFAULT row in case a subject doesn't have its own specific row
  5. Check totals: Make sure the three percentages add up to 100%

6.9 "Access Denied" Messages

Problem: You see messages saying you don't have access or permission.

Try these solutions:

  1. Check your Google account: Make sure you're logged into the correct Google account (usually your school email)
  2. Ask for permission: Contact your administrator and ask them to give you edit access to the spreadsheet
  3. Check Drive permission: Make sure you have permission to create files in Google Drive
  4. Log out and back in: Sign out of Google and sign back in
  5. Contact support: If the problem continues, contact your systems developer

6.10 My Changes Didn't Save

Problem: You made changes but they disappeared or didn't save.

Try these solutions:

  1. Check internet: Make sure you have a stable internet connection
  2. Try again: Close the form, refresh the page, and try making the change again
  3. Check permissions: Make sure you have write/edit permission for the spreadsheet
  4. Wait a moment: Sometimes it takes a few seconds for changes to save - give it a moment
  5. Contact support: If changes keep disappearing, contact your systems developer

6.11 System Not Reading My Data

Problem: You added information to a reference sheet but it's not showing up in the forms.

Try these solutions:

  1. Check header rows: Reference sheets need to have exactly 2 header rows at the top - make sure you didn't accidentally delete or add rows
  2. Check Active column location: The Active column should be the last column (rightmost) in the sheet
  3. Check data placement: Your actual data should start from row 3 (because rows 1 and 2 are headers)
  4. Check spelling: Column names must be spelled correctly
  5. Check for extra spaces: Make sure there are no extra spaces before or after your data
  6. Refresh the form: Close and reopen the form after making changes

6.12 System is Running Slowly

Problem: When you click buttons, it takes a long time to process, or the system seems slow.

What's happening:

  • The system handles multiple items at once (called "batch operations") to be more efficient
  • This is normal and expected

What to do:

  1. Be patient: Wait for the operation to complete - the button will show progress (like "Adding X assignments...")
  2. Don't click multiple times: Clicking the button multiple times won't make it faster and might cause problems
  3. Check internet speed: A slow internet connection can make everything slower
  4. Work in smaller batches: If you're adding or deleting many items at once, try doing it in smaller groups
  5. Let it finish: Don't close the form or refresh the page while it's processing

This guide covers everything you need to know to use the Template Masterfile system. If you need additional help, please contact your systems developer.
